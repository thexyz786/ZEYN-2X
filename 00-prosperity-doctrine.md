## 1. The objective function

The doctrine does not treat profit as the terminal goal. It treats **barakat** —
increase that endures and multiplies beyond its input — as the goal, and profit
as its measurable residue. The homepage frames the target as prosperity in
**dīn** *and* **dunyā** simultaneously, and repeats the invocation
"Barakat. Barakat. Barakat." under the tagline *A Journey of Prosperity*.

**Operational consequence.** A move that raises this quarter's profit while
degrading trust, ethics, or relational capital is scored as a *loss* under this
objective function, not a gain. `/grow-a-business/` states it directly:
compromising ethical values carries "detrimental long-term repercussions," while
upholding them builds trust and credibility and is what actually secures
long-run success.

This is the only definition of growth that is genuinely unbounded. Extractive
growth hits a ceiling when the counterparty pool is exhausted. Trust-compounding
growth does not, because each honoured transaction enlarges the pool.

## 2. The governing directive

The organisation's motto, attributed to **Syedna Mufaddal Saifuddin TUS**:

> "Ply your trade and business according to the demands of this day and age."

Read precisely, this is a **standing obligation to modernise**. It converts
adaptation from optional strategy into religious instruction. A business that
runs on last decade's methods is not merely uncompetitive under this frame — it
is out of compliance with the directive.

Paired instruction: gain business excellence by acquiring acumen through
education, **and more importantly** through the experience and knowledge held by
your elders (`/business/the-importance-of-seeking-business-advice-tips-for-entrepreneurs/`).
Note the ranking — transmitted experience is placed *above* formal education.
That is a cheap, high-yield input most founders under-use.

## 3. The vocational premise

**Syedna Mohammed Burhanuddin RA:** "Allah Taʿālā has designated all of you as a
business community" (`/grow-a-business/`), and — on employment — do not remain
service-minded for life; "now is the high time to become business-minded"
(`/start-business`).

Enterprise is framed here as the default vocation, not the risky exception.
Salaried work is treated as a stage to transit through, and there is a named
course for the transit ("Service to Business Transition," five steps).

**Industry carries a higher weighting than trade.** `/idd/` reports Syedna
Mohammed Burhanuddin RA holding that industries "ensure the welfare of Allah
Taʿālā's beneficiaries, hence, yield greater divine reward." Manufacturing and
industrial ventures are explicitly elevated above pure trading — because they
create employment and supply rather than merely intermediating.

**Trajectory implication:** trading → manufacturing is the doctrinally-preferred
direction of travel, not a lateral move.

## 4. The six inclusive practices

Stated verbatim as principles on `/aboutus/` and `/about`:

| # | Principle | Substance as stated |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Ethicality & Integrity** | Honesty, transparency, ethical conduct in all practice |
| 2 | **Regular Education & Innovation** | Continuous growth, adaptation, acceptance of innovation to stay relevant |
| 3 | **Community & Collaboration** | Cooperation and mutual support; group effort out-yields individual effort |
| 4 | **Diversity & Inclusion** | Diverse perspectives and backgrounds; equity and equal opportunity |
| 5 | **Sustainable Growth** | Long-term stability prioritised; negative externalities reduced |
| 6 | **Support & Empowerment** | Tailored, transformative guidance via skilled counselling |

The stated formula binding them: **Shariyat Mohammediyah + modern business
practice**. Neither half alone. The doctrine is explicitly not traditionalist —
it demands contemporary method — and explicitly not amoral — it demands the
ethical frame that method operates inside.

## 5. Ethics as identity, not compliance

The critical distinction in this doctrine, and the one with the most strategic
weight: ethics is framed as "an essential component of our faith," not as a
regulatory burden or a marketing position
(`/business/the-importance-of-seeking-business-advice-tips-for-entrepreneurs/`).

A compliance-framed ethic degrades under pressure, because compliance is a cost
to be minimised. An identity-framed ethic strengthens under pressure, because
abandoning it costs more than the transaction is worth. Only the second kind
compounds — and compounding is the whole thesis.

The commercial expression of this: the DBohra portal sells access to "a verified
community of ethical business practitioners" (`/dbohra/`). Reputation has been
made into infrastructure. Ethics is the moat.

## 6. Obligation flows downward as well as up

`/business/the-duties-of-an-employer-towards-their-employees-during-ashara-mubaraka/`
is the clearest statement that the ethical frame binds the employer, not just
the trader:

- Operations pause for all ten days of Ashara Mubaraka — an edict, not a
  discretionary holiday.
- The duty extends to **non-mumin staff**: explain the observance, communicate
  dates precisely, and do so "in a clear, kind and effective manner." The manner
  of communication is itself part of the obligation.
- Duties to mumin staff include enabling attendance, assisting with religious
  attire, and facilitating **Qardan Hasana** — interest-free loans.
- Staff are to be trained that no emergency justifies breaking the closure.

**Syedna Mohammed Burhanuddin RA** on the mutual-aid basis for all of this:
consider a Mumin your brother; assist him with opinion, idea, counsel,
instruction, and financial aid (`/business-counseling/`). This is the stated
theological grounding for why the counselling network is free or subsidised.

## 7. Where this sits — the institution above the doctrine

*(Added rev 2, from the dbdea.org transcript — this resolves what was an open
question in rev 1.)*

**DBDEA = the Dawoodi Bohra Department of Economic Affairs = Umoor Iqtesadiyah.**
It is the parent. Tijaarat Raabehah is one of **four arms** beneath it, not the
whole apparatus. Rev 1 inferred this relationship from an Instagram handle;
it is now stated outright.

| Arm | What it actually is |
|---|---|
| **Tijaarat al Raabehah** | Industry and trade development — programmes, certification of business service providers, network building |
| **Qardan Hasana** | The **capital** layer. Interest-free financing for enterprise, housing, education, medical need and welfare, governed by Sharia principles |
| **The Saifee Foundation** | MSME support, small-business funding, skills development, microfinance — long-standing focus on low-income households and women's entrepreneurship |
| **DBohra Business Solutions** | Trade facilitation and professional services: sustainable energy, strategic consultancy, corporate services, industrial development, business travel, HR, digital transformation |

Plus **Basateen** — financial and non-financial assistance to help families
establish sustainable livelihoods and rebuild through meaningful work.

**Why this reframes everything downstream.** Rev 1 read the doctrine as a
teaching-and-counselling system with capital mentioned in passing. It is not.
It is a **four-layer stack — doctrine, capital, subsidy, and distribution —
and the capital layer reports 50,000+ interest-free cases facilitated.** A
founder reading only the Tijaarat Raabehah half is reading the curriculum and
missing the balance sheet. See Register 05.

### Heritage as stated
The community traces its heritage to the Fatimi Imams — descendants of Prophet
Mohammed (peace be upon him) — who established a flourishing intellectual and
economic civilisation in Egypt. Under the 53rd al-Dai al-Mutlaq, **Syedna
Mufaddal Saifuddin TUS**, it is described as a global network of over half a
million members across 40+ countries.

### Governance shape
**Central Office** sets strategic direction, standards and shared resources.
**Zonal Business Councils (ZBCs)** adapt and implement those frameworks to
regional need — running training and acceleration programmes, financial
facilitation and advisory, and on-ground entrepreneurship initiatives. The
stated design is consistency in purpose and governance, flexibility in
execution, alignment between global vision and local reality.

**Operationally this matters more than it sounds.** It means the access point is
not necessarily Mumbai. There is a regional layer by design.

## 8. Petition as a step in the method

`/` and `/home-old/` place a **duʿāʾ ʿaraḍ** to Ḥaḍrat ʿĀliyah alongside the
practical toolkit — not as sentiment appended to strategy but as a listed step
in seeking prosperity. Likewise, a partnership deed is not final at signature:
it is presented at Ḥaḍrat ʿĀliyah and issued as **Misaal Shareef** (see `02`).
Commercial instruments are routed through religious authority by design.
