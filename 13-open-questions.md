## Tier 1 — blocking (five)

### Q1. What is your work status in the United States?
Citizen, permanent resident, F-1 student, or something else. Nothing in the
legal stack can be settled without this.

- **Citizen or permanent resident:** you can operate the business yourself;
  SBA-backed programmes and small-business certifications are open.
- **F-1:** you may *own* a business but may not *work* in it without
  authorisation (CPT/OPT). Actively selling, assembling and delivering is work.
  This changes the design to one where a work-authorised person operates and
  you own, or the venture waits for OPT. Verify with the Pierce College
  international student office and an immigration attorney — see `11` §8.

Why it changes the work: entity choice, who signs invoices, who may be paid,
which government programmes apply, and whether Fork A can start this quarter.

### Q2. R5 — which route through the industry-experience rule?
The base makes 6–12 months inside the industry a pre-launch requirement
(`01` R5). You have none in gifting. Pick one:

| Route | What it is | Doctrine | Speed | Cost |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **(a) Job + pilot** | Take a seasonal Q4 role at an LA gifting, floral or fulfilment operation; run a capped pilot alongside | Compliant | First invoice Nov–Dec | Your hours — the role eats most of the 10–15/week |
| **(b) Pilot as apprenticeship** | The first five orders *are* the apprenticeship; each capped so a mistake costs under $100; logged in `01` Incident Log as a knowing deviation | Deviation, recorded | First invoice October | Learning paid for with your own small orders |
| **(c) Wait** | Work inside the industry through 2027; launch Q4 2027 | Compliant | Twelve months lost | Opportunity cost |

My recommendation is (a) if a seasonal role exists within reach of campus,
otherwise (b) with the deviation written down. This is your rule; your call.

### Q3. Where exactly is the community line?
"Do not sell to the Bohras" can mean two different things:

- **Narrow:** no selling to community members as *consumers*.
- **Broad:** no selling to Bohra-*owned businesses* either, even when the
  recipients are their non-Bohra employees.

The broad reading closes the warmest B2B pool you have — owners who already
trust you and have staff. The narrow reading keeps it open while still not
trading on community goodwill for personal gifts. Which is it, and why? If the
reason is "I don't want to be seen as using the community", the narrow line
plus a stated ask ("I want introductions, not orders") usually protects that
better than refusing their business does.

### Q4. Vizag — who operates there, and does the America-first amendment stand?
Two sub-questions, one decision.

1. During Diwali and Q4 — the only gifting season that matters in India — you
   are in Los Angeles. Who in Vizag would take an order, source it, pack it and
   deliver it? A named family member under a **written deed** (`02`, R4), or
   nobody until you are physically there in summer 2027?
2. `05` was amended on 2026-08-27 to America-first, India only if US traction
   appears. Today's brief says both. Do you (i) reaffirm America-first and treat
   Vizag as a *sourcing and design base* only, or (ii) strike the amendment and
   run Vizag as a *selling market* from Diwali 2027 with a named operator?

Sensitivity to state plainly: your father's role as Aamil is the community's
trust position in that city. His involvement in a commercial venture, even
informally, carries reputational weight for him and the venture. That argues
for a deed, a clear separation, and his counsel rather than his labour.

## Tier 2 — sharpening

- **Fork B reframed.** Research in `11` §9 finds the student *consumer* at a
  commuter community college is a weak buyer. The buyers around a campus are
  student organisations, parents at milestones, departments through foundation
  funds, and — after transfer — residential care-package demand. Do you accept
  that Fork B is a **resource fork now** (credibility, mentors, cheap tools,
  interns, a pilot with one student org) and a **revenue fork after transfer**?
- **Brand.** Is the venture name decided? The repo is `ZEYN-2X`; nothing in the
  base names the business. A name is needed for the DBA filing and the domain.
- **Food.** Year one with packaged food from licensed local makers (allergens
  labelled), or non-food only? Food raises margins and complexity together.
- **Delivery capacity.** Do you have a car in LA? Hand delivery inside Warner
  Center is the cheapest differentiator against shipped boxes.
- **Pilot #1.** Is there any business you already have a relationship with —
  an employer, an internship, a family friend's firm — that could be the first
  paid order? The first invoice is a relationship, not a campaign.
- **Transfer.** Which university and when? It sets the Fork B timeline and
  which entrepreneurship centre becomes available.
- **Banking.** Do you hold a US bank account in your own name, and an SSN or
  ITIN? Ties to Q1; determines how payments are received.
- **Father's counsel.** Is he willing to be asked — for counsel and for
  introductions — given his position? R6 ranks his experience above anything
  in `11`.
