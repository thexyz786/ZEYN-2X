# docs/doctrine/ — the ethics and principles base

**Stated by the operator, 2 September 2026: these files are the base for the
venture's principles and ethics.** Not background reading — the standard the
business is held to.

Business doctrine extracted from **tijaaratraabehah.org** and **dbdea.org**
(Dawoodi Bohra Department of Economic Affairs / Umoor Iqtesadiyah), structured
as a living system rather than a summary.

**The premise everything else hangs on:** the ethics layer and the growth layer
are not two layers. Ethics is not the constraint on growth — it is the mechanism
of growth, because the only increase that compounds without a ceiling is the
kind that enlarges trust with every transaction. That is what *barakat* names.
**Argue from that premise, not from restraint.**

## How to use them

**Consult; never recite.** `MASTER.md` §6 is the operative compilation — the
doctrine already reduced to what governs this venture in Los Angeles. Read these
registers for provenance, for a citation, or when §6 is too compressed to settle
a question.

Two standing instructions from `MASTER.md` §1:

- **§1.3, the doctrine test.** Before advising on any deal, partnership,
  supplier relationship or capital decision, test it against the Conduct Code and
  Partnership Law and **name the rule it touches**. "This is an R3 question"
  beats a paragraph of hedging.
- **§1.2, the jurisdiction rule.** The machinery here is India-shaped — rupee
  figures, GST, Indian registration, community capital access. Treat every one as
  unavailable until proven otherwise and give the US equivalent instead.
  **The doctrine transfers; the machinery does not.**
