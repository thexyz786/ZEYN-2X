## The correction this file exists to make

Registers `00`–`03` and `06` at rev 1 read Tijaarat Raabehah as the whole
apparatus. It is not. It is one of **four arms** of the Dawoodi Bohra Department
of Economic Affairs (**DBDEA** / Umoor Iqtesadiyah). Read only the Tijaarat half
and you have the curriculum without the balance sheet.

| Arm | Layer | What it gives you |
|---|---|---|
| **Tijaarat al Raabehah** | Doctrine & capability | Programmes, certification of business service providers, network building |
| **Qardan Hasana** | **Capital** | Interest-free financing — enterprise, housing, education, medical, welfare. Sharia-governed |
| **The Saifee Foundation** | Subsidy | MSME support, small-business funding, skills development, microfinance. Focus on low-income households and women's entrepreneurship |
| **DBohra Business Solutions** | Distribution & services | Trade facilitation, professional services, market growth support, business management consultancy |

**Basateen** — a further initiative offering financial *and non-financial*
assistance to help families establish sustainable livelihoods.

## Qardan Hasana — the instrument that matters most to you

Interest-free financial assistance to individuals and households, paired with
knowledge, skill-building and mentorship. Reported scale: **50,000+ cases
facilitated**.

**Stated use cases** — the first three are the ones that concern a founder:
- Starting new businesses
- Investing in existing businesses
- Building sustainable income streams
- Essential household requirements
- Education-related needs

**Operating principles as published:** fair assessment and allocation ·
responsible use of funds · structured repayment · transparent process ·
accountability on *both* sides — provider and recipient.

It also explicitly aims to build financial awareness, responsible
decision-making and disciplined use of resources. In other words the money
comes with a discipline requirement attached, which is consistent with `01/R1`,
`R2` and `R3`.

**Access:** Book a consultation. `qardanhasana.org` · `counseling.dbohra.com`

## The occupational gap — the most useful number on the site

Published occupational mix of ~**116,617** members in business, industry and
the professions:

| Segment | Share |
|---|---|
| Retailers | 43.4% |
| Self Employed | 22.5% |
| Wholesalers | 12.8% |
| Manufacturers | 8.9% |
| Distributors | 6.4% |
| Home Industry | 6.0% |

**Read this against `00 §3`.** The doctrine holds that **industry yields greater
reward than trade** — and only **8.9%** of the community are manufacturers,
against **43.4%** retailers. Roughly **79%** of the community sits in retail,
self-employment and wholesale: the intermediating end.

The position the doctrine ranks highest is the position that is emptiest. That
is not a coincidence to admire — it is a gap to enter. Whatever else is true,
the competitive density at the bottom of the ladder is five times the density at
the top, inside a network that is doctrinally predisposed to support the top.

*(The reading is mine. The percentages are theirs.)*

## The access architecture — and the part that concerns you

**Central Office** sets strategy, standards and shared resources.
**Zonal Business Councils (ZBCs)** implement regionally — training and
acceleration programmes, financial facilitation and advisory, on-ground
entrepreneurship initiatives. Stated design: consistency in governance,
flexibility in execution, alignment between global vision and local reality.

**The operational consequence:** the entry point is regional by design, not
Mumbai by default. **40+ countries of operation.** Whether a ZBC covers
Los Angeles is the single question worth asking first.

### The honest tension

Your stated position is to use a few individual community contacts to get
moving, stay out of jamaat institutions wholesale, and shift to the general
public once traction is real. That position and this file are not in conflict —
but only if you separate two different kinds of engagement:

**Transactional — take it.** Qardan Hasana, Saifee Foundation funding, Trade
Desk introductions, DBohra listing, a counselling call. You receive a specific
thing, you repay or reciprocate on defined terms, and you are done. There is no
standing obligation and no committee. Refusing non-dilutive interest-free
capital on the grounds that its source is institutional is a decision that costs
real money for an aesthetic reason.

**Institutional — think twice.** ZBC membership, committee roles, certification
as a service provider, anything with an ongoing governance relationship. This
is what you described as a mess. It probably is. It also has a way of expanding
to fill available time, and time is the input you have least of while studying.

**The line:** take the capital, take the introductions, decline the committee.
