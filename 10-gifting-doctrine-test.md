## 2. The venture as briefed — fact, assumption, conflict

**Stated by the operator, 2026-09-02 (fact):**

- A gifting business, sold as a **service**, started small.
- Fork A: sell to **businesses**, on a holiday calendar, for their employees.
- Fork B: sell to **students** at Los Angeles Pierce College now, and at the
  future university after transfer.
- Two theatres: **Los Angeles** (academic life) and **Visakhapatnam** (family
  base for the past year; father serves as Aamil there).
- Contacts sourced through the Dawoodi Bohra community, where the operator has
  good standing. **No selling to the community.**
- Long run: companies across America.
- Government establishments (SBA and the like) used to advantage.
- Build step by step; research first; questions before commitment.

**Assumed until the operator says otherwise (assumption):**

- Solo operator; roughly 10–15 hours a week during the semester.
- No outside capital; no hiring; no app.
- "Service" means curation, sourcing, assembly and delivery — not manufacturing.
- US work/immigration status is unknown. Everything in the legal stack
  branches on it. *(Open question 1 in `13`.)*

**Where the brief and the base pull against each other (conflict):**

## 3. The test — rule by rule

| Rule | What it means for a gifting service | Status |
|---|---|---|
| **Objective — barakat** (`00 §1`) | Every box reaches an employee who did not choose the vendor. Each honest box enlarges the pool: the recipient is next year's buyer at their own firm. Design for the *recipient's* trust — quality they can feel, a discreet card — not for flattering the buyer. Growth mechanism, not marketing. | Clear |
| **Modernise** (`00 §2`) | A CRM, a digital lookbook, online prepayment from the first order. But Rung 1 says *cheap digital tools*: forms and invoices, not a platform. | Clear |
| **Industry > trade** (`00 §3`) | Curated gifting is intermediation — Rung 1 trade. Not a flaw; a starting rung. The ladder direction is toward *making*: own-label items, eventually produced in Vizag. Horizon, not launch. | Clear — horizon |
| **Diversity & inclusion** (`00 §4`) | A multi-faith occasion calendar (Diwali, Eid, Lunar New Year, Hanukkah, Christmas, Nowruz) for LA's mixed workforces is doctrine *and* the differentiator the enterprise platforms are weakest at. The operator's bicultural position is the credential. | Clear |
| **Employer duties** (`00 §6`, `01`) | Bind at the first hire. Before that: the ten-day Ashara closure goes on the delivery calendar from day one and no delivery is ever promised across it. Muharram 1449 begins around the first week of June 2027 (computed from 1 Muharram 1447 ≈ 26 June 2025, minus ~11 days a year — verify against the community's Misri calendar each year). Off-season for gifting; it drifts earlier each year and reaches Q4 only in the 2040s. | Clear |
| **R1** spend less than you earn | The venture must never draw on living money. Personal cash into it is capped in advance and replenished only from revenue. | Needs a number — `13` Q5 |
| **R2** save a third | One third of net profit to a reserve account, untouched for twelve months. From the first invoice, not "once it's bigger". | Clear |
| **R3** cash, not credit | Customers **prepay**. Nothing is bought until it is sold. No net-30 in year one; a buyer who insists gets a deposit that covers cost of goods, or a polite no. No SBA 7(a), no microloan, no card float. If capital is ever needed it is `05` capital at 0% — Qardan Hasana via the LA jamaat, JFLA, Kiva. | Clear — expect buyer friction; price the service to justify prepay |
| **R4** every partnership in writing, including family | Any Vizag role for a parent or sibling is a partnership. Written deed (Register `02`), structure named, **before** the first rupee or hour. Same for any LA friend who helps assemble or sell. | Needs a decision — `13` Q4 |
| **R5** 6–12 months inside first | **The operator has not worked inside gifting.** Routes: (a) a seasonal Q4 role at an LA gifting or fulfilment company while running a tiny pilot; (b) the pilot itself as the apprenticeship, capped so any mistake costs under $100; (c) delay to Q4 2027. Doctrine prefers (a) or (c). Speed prefers (b). | **Conflict** — `13` Q2 |
| **R6** counsel; elders above education | Four conversations before the first sales call: the father, as Aamil and elder; two Bohra business owners in LA who buy gifts for staff; one SCORE mentor. Plus the free Tijaarat Raabehah discovery call. Logged in `05` access log. | Clear |
| **R7** never trade ethics for a transaction | Gifting has named traps: gifts to healthcare referral sources (anti-kickback); "handmade" or "artisan" claims that are not verified; describing gift cards as tax-free to the employee; unlabelled allergens; alcohol. And the community boundary: ask contacts for introductions with the ask stated plainly, so no one later feels used. | Clear — lines listed in §4 |
| **Partnership law** (`02`) | Vizag: if a parent funds and the operator directs → Mudaarabat. If a sibling operates locally and the operator directs → the split of expertise must be written or it is not a Mudaarabat. Deed register entry either way. | Pending decision |
| **Ladder Rung 0** (`03`) | Readiness on three axes. Personal: semester hours. Financial: cap. Professional: zero industry months — see R5. | Two of three ready |
| **Ladder Rung 1** (`03`) | Validate through customer conversations *before* building product. Twenty conversations before a lookbook is final. | Clear — it is the plan |
| **Capital** (`05`) | Prepay means no capital need at launch. This is the correct outcome, not a limitation. | Clear |
| **Jurisdiction** (README §1.2) | Two regimes now, not one. US side: India machinery unavailable, US equivalents given (`11`). India side: the father is an Aamil in Vizag — the deed route in `02` step 1 runs *through an Aamil Saheb*; the machinery is reachable there in a way the base did not anticipate. Keep the two theatres in separate registers. | Amend the base when Q4 is answered |
| **America-first amendment** (`05`, 2026-08-27) | Superseded or reaffirmed by the operator — not by this file. | Awaiting `13` Q4 |

## 4. Standing rules the venture runs on (derived, provisional)

Numbered G1–G8 so they can be cited like R1–R7. Each traces to a rule above.

| # | Rule | Source |
|---|---|---|
| **G1** | **Prepay.** 100% before any purchase. Annual plans: each occasion prepaid at least 30 days before delivery. | R3 |
| **G2** | **No inventory.** Buy to order. The only stock is samples, and samples come out of G3. | R3, `03` Rung 1 |
| **G3** | **Float cap.** Personal cash into the venture is capped at a stated figure (proposed $500), logged to the dollar, replenished only from revenue. | R1 |
| **G4** | **Reserve.** One third of net profit to a separate account; not touched for twelve months. | R2 |
| **G5** | **Deed first.** No family member or friend puts in money or hours before a written deed with a named structure. | R4, `02` |
| **G6** | **Counsel first.** Four counsel conversations logged before the first sales call. | R6 |
| **G7** | **Ethics lines.** No gifts to healthcare referral sources. No alcohol. No origin or "handmade" claim that cannot be shown. Allergens labelled on every food item. Gift cards only with the written tax note to the buyer. Community contacts asked for introductions, never sold to, and told so. | R7 |
| **G8** | **Calendar.** Ashara closure marked before the year's first order. No delivery promised across it. | `00 §6` |
